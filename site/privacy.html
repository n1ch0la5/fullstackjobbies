Privacy, in plain English

Short version: we don't sell anything about you, we don't run ads, and most of what you do here never leaves your browser. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Checking a job

When you paste a job description into the hero box or the Red Flag Detector, it's rated right there in your browser. We never receive the text.

When you paste a job link, our server fetches that page so it can be rated, then hands the result back to you. We don't store the link or the page. Our hosting provider, Netlify, may keep short-lived technical logs of requests, as most web hosts do.

Votes and live emojis

To stop one person voting fifty times, your browser makes up a random ID the first time you vote. We store that ID with your vote. It isn't linked to your name, email or anything else about you. Emoji taps are only counted; we don't record who tapped. This data is kept with Supabase, our database provider.

Email signups

If you sign up for the newsletter, the agent API updates, or employer news, we keep your email address and use it only for that. Forms are handled by Netlify. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to see which pages and features people use, so we know what's worth building. It uses cookies. If you're in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, nothing loads until you click "Sure" on our cookie banner, and you can change your mind from the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page. We don't use advertising cookies.

Stored in your browser

A few conveniences live only in your own browser: the countries you picked on the jobs page, your cookie choice, and which jobs you've voted on. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The browser extension

The Full Stack Jobbies extension rates job pages entirely inside your browser. It doesn't send page contents, your browsing or anything else to us or anyone, and it has no analytics. It only reads a page when it's a job page on a supported hiring site, or when you right-click selected text and choose "Rate this job." It asks for these permissions: context menus (for the right-click option), active tab and scripting (to show the rating on the page you're on, only when you ask), and storage (to save your preferences).

If you set preferences in the extension (work style, salary floor, countries, interviews to avoid), they're saved in Chrome's own storage, which Chrome syncs between your browsers if you have Chrome sync turned on. They're used only on your device to show the "For you" check, and are never sent to us.

The jobs we list

Listings come from companies' public job boards and public job feeds. We show facts, our rating and a link to apply on the company's own site. If you represent a company and want a listing corrected or removed, email us.
