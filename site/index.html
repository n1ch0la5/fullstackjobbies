The Jobbie Scale

Every listing gets a starting rating from what it actually says. Developers who applied, interviewed or just read it then vote it up or down. Six levels, no fence-sitting.

LEVEL 5

Rocket ship 🚀

Top-band salary, two rounds, real remote. Apply before lunch.

LEVEL 4

Solid 💯

Fair pay, sane process, replies to every applicant.

LEVEL 3

Meh 😐

A job. It exists. Nobody's excited, nobody's harmed.

LEVEL 2

Bit whiffy 🤢

Salary's there but low. Stack list reads like a shopping trolley.

LEVEL 1

Steaming pile 💩

"Competitive salary", eight rounds, unpaid take-home, and a ghosting habit.

LEVEL 0

Bloody awful ☠️

Unpaid "trial week", equity instead of salary, and the recruiter ghosted mid-call.
